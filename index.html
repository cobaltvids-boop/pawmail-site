What PawMail is

Add your dog or cat, then keep up with three small missions a day — a walk or play session, a care check, a quick training rep. Once a day your pet calls and leaves you a short, silly voicemail: about their day, the water bowl, the walk you took earlier, or how much they miss you. Keep a streak going and they call to celebrate. It's for fun and for building good habits — not veterinary advice.

Questions

Does my pet really call?

Your pet can't use a phone (we've checked). Every message is written by us and recorded in advance in a cast of character voices, then picked for your pet — by the personality you chose, their streak and what you've done together today. Calls arrive once a day at a random time in the window you set in Settings, and land in your Voicemails inbox.

Is my photo stored?

Not by us. If you add a portrait, the photo — with your pet's name, species, breed and age — goes to our server and on to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes your pet's Report Card. Our server doesn't save it or write it to its logs. Anthropic keeps what it receives for a limited time under its own policy. The photo itself stays on your phone. The full privacy policy has the details.

How do I delete my data?

Everything PawMail keeps lives on your phone: your pet's profile and photo, missions, walks, streaks and voicemails. To erase it, go to Settings → Start over, or delete the app. There are no accounts, so there's nothing on our side to delete.
