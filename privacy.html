Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026

The short version

  • No accounts. No ads. No tracking. No analytics.
  • Everything PawMail keeps lives on your phone.
  • One thing leaves it: your pet's portrait, if you add one, so we can write their Report Card. Our server passes it to Anthropic and doesn't keep it.
  • Delete the app, or use Settings → Start over, and it's all gone.

What stays on your phone

Your pet's profile (name, species, breed, age, gender and the personality you picked), their portrait, missions, walks, streaks and badges, your voicemail inbox, and your settings. It's stored only on your device. We can't see it, and there's no copy on any server.

What leaves your phone, and why

Your pet's portrait — only if you add one

When you add a portrait, the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our server along with your pet's name, species, breed and age, and the last few Report Card headlines (so a new one isn't repeated). Our server forwards it to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes the Report Card, and sends the result back to your phone.

  • Our server doesn't save the photo or those details, and doesn't write them to its logs. It handles the request and forgets it.
  • Anthropic receives the photo and details to produce the Report Card and retains them for a limited period under its own policy. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • None of it is linked to you: there's no account, email or device identifier attached.
  • In App Store terms: Photos and Other User Content (your pet's details), used for app functionality, not linked to you, not used for tracking.

You can use PawMail without ever adding a portrait.

What doesn't leave your phone

  • Voicemails. Every message is recorded in advance and built into the app. Nothing is generated about you, and nothing is sent anywhere to play one.
  • Notifications. Your pet's daily call is scheduled on your phone as a local notification. There's no push server and no notification token.
  • Siri. "Start a walk in PawMail" just opens the app and starts the timer.
  • Sharing. A voicemail or card is only shared when you tap Share, and only to the app or person you choose.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Start over to erase your pet and everything about them, or delete PawMail from your phone. Because there are no accounts and our server keeps nothing, there's nothing else to delete. For data Anthropic retains, see its policy linked above.

Children

PawMail doesn't ask for anyone's personal information, adult or child. A portrait should be of your pet.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If what leaves your phone ever changes, the app will tell you before it happens.
